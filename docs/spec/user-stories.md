# User stories

Draft consolidated from the user-confirmed workflow. Each story refers to acceptance criteria in the [product specification](requirements.md).

## U01 — Develop an experiment with AI

As a home brewer, I discuss an external recipe with ChatGPT using my coffees, equipment, previous brews, taste observations, and relevant internet sources. I can ask whether it suits my tastes and adapt it for roast level and serving quantity. When satisfied, I say “save this” and it is stored as an experimental recipe with sources and adaptation notes.

Acceptance reference: R06, R12.

## U02 — Select and adjust an experiment

On brew day, I open Coffee Lab on my phone and select an experiment or Keeper. A saved coffee-specific variant can supply starting settings for a new bag of the same coffee. I can adjust settings for this execution without editing the base recipe. If an adjustment works well, I ask ChatGPT or use the app to save it as a reusable variant, linked to the base recipe, coffee, and originating execution.

Acceptance reference: R01, R06, R08.

## U03 — Brew from readable instructions

I follow clear recipe steps on my phone with screen-awake support and use my scale for timing. Instructions and targets are readable during execution.

When brewing a familiar Keeper, I can simply read and brew without creating a record or completing a logging task. I can choose to record an execution when trying an experiment, changing the recipe, or noticing a different result. I decide when a recipe deserves Keeper status.

Acceptance reference: R07.

## U04 — Record results and revisit the cup

I attach photos of the bed, scale, and drink and record quantities, elapsed time, and observations through ChatGPT or Coffee Lab. I expect to use ChatGPT more often. An explicit save request saves the discussed content immediately. I can add a description, optional sensory scales, or a quick overall rating later.

Each brew retains its own recipe snapshot, planned targets, and execution data.

Acceptance reference: R08, R10, R12.

## U05 — Record a casual untracked brew

I can say “My wife made an AeroPress yesterday using this bag.” Selecting the standard recipe supplies its execution quantities for usage statistics. The entry is complete, marked untracked, and retains the selected recipe snapshot.

Acceptance reference: R09.

## U06 — Add bags and equipment

I can supply several packaging photos to ChatGPT, add purchase details, and ask it to save the bag and original photos. I can enter and correct the same details in Coffee Lab. I can maintain my equipment library, attach photos, and record the grinder setting used.

Acceptance reference: R04, R05, R12.

## U07 — Review the journal

I find recorded executions by coffee, bag, base recipe, variant, method, date, and equipment. I compare a recipe's results, photos, observations, and ratings and ask ChatGPT questions using my saved history. Usage totals are secondary summaries of recorded executions. Finished bags and retired items remain accessible through history and searches.

For example, I compare Hoffmann with Café do Vô Zico and Stardust at the same grinder setting, then compare two Stardust executions with different grind settings. If a 1:17 execution tastes better than 1:15, I can save it as a Stardust variant and reuse it as the starting point for a future bag. ChatGPT's assessment refers to the recorded examples and acknowledges other changed settings or missing observations.

Acceptance reference: R11, R12, R14.

## U08 — Keep independent copies

I export my account records and original photos. I can also export a full recipe or the currently filtered journal as a readable PDF, with photos included by default. Automated backups and tested recovery protect the hosted records and images.

Acceptance reference: R13, R15.

## U09 — Control account access and preferences

I sign in through email/password or Google to the same account. As owner, I manage authorized email addresses. I choose English or pt-BR and select weight and temperature units independently.

Acceptance reference: R02, R03.
