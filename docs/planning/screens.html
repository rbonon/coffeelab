Coffee Lab planning

Screens

The proposed screen plan puts finding and reading a recipe first, with optional execution logging.

  • Phone navigation: Home, Recipes, Journal, and Library; account settings remain secondary.
  • Recipe detail leads to focused reading without starting a timer or requiring a brew record.
  • Journal and comparison keep coffee, bag, recipe variant, planned targets, and actual results distinct.
  • Desktop layouts use the additional space for browsing and side-by-side comparisons.
