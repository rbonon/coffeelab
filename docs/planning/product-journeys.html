Coffee Lab planning

Product journeys

Recipe selection and reading are primary. Brewing a Keeper can end without a journal entry or completion task.

  1. Find a Keeper, read its instructions, and brew without mandatory logging.
  2. Run an experiment and optionally save results, photos, and tasting observations.
  3. Save successful execution settings as a coffee-specific variant.
  4. Use that variant as a starting point for a new bag of the same coffee.
  5. Discuss recipes or results in ChatGPT and save on an explicit request.
  6. Enter or correct the same records directly in Coffee Lab.
